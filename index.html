Notes & assumptions

  • Epoch uses **TBC-era coefficients**; heals crit for 150% (expected multiplier 1 + 0.5 × crit% for direct heals).
  • Pre-20 rank penalty: 1 − 0.0375 × (20 − learnLvl). Downrank penalty: min(1, (learnLvl+11)/playerLevel).
  • Coefficients used: HT 1.00; Rejuv 0.80 (0.20/tick ×4); Regrowth 0.1865 direct + 0.6363 HoT; Lifebloom ≈0.67 HoT total + 0.50 bloom; Tranquility 0.33 per target.
  • ToL aura adds a flat 0.25 × Spirit to each heal event when toggled.
