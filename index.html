Notes & assumptions

  • Default level 60. Direct heals crit for 150% → EV multiplier 1 + 0.5 × crit%.
  • Downranking: Pre-20 penalty 1 − 0.0375 × (20 − learnLvl); old-rank factor min(1, (learnLvl+11)/level).
  • Spirit regen (2.4): MP5_spirit = 5 * (0.001 + 0.010999 * Spirit * sqrt(Int)). Intensity lets 30% of that work while casting; MP5 (gear) always works.
  • Coefficients: HT 1.00; Rejuv 0.80 (0.20/tick ×4); Regrowth 0.1865 direct + 0.6363 HoT; LB ≈0.67 HoT + 0.50 bloom; Tranq 0.33 per target.
  • New column: Casts in Fight (regen) = min(time-limited casts, mana-limited casts with regen).
